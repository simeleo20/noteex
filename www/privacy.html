Privacy Policy — Noteex

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Noteex (com.simelab.noteex) is developed by SimeLab. This policy explains what data the app handles. Short version: none.

What we collect

Nothing. Noteex does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party services embedded in the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you explicitly enable Microphone mode. Audio from your microphone is processed in real time on your device solely to detect note pitch, and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. When you leave Microphone mode, the app releases the microphone immediately.

Data stored on your device

Your practice settings (clef, range, answer mode) and your score history are saved locally on your device only. They never leave your device and are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Microphone: optional, requested only when you use Microphone mode.

Children

The app contains no content inappropriate for children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date above.
