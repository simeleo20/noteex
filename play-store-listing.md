## Full description

```
Most apps for learning to read music stop at one clef and a fixed range. Noteex was built because no existing app offers this many functions and customizations in one place — free, offline, with no ads.

PRACTICE YOUR WAY
• Four clefs: treble, bass, alto and tenor
• Any note range you like, from low C1 to high C8
• Answer with buttons, in Do Re Mi or C D E letters

USE YOUR VOICE OR A REAL INSTRUMENT
• Microphone mode detects the pitch of what you sing or play
• Relative mode: any octave of the right pitch class counts
• Exact mode: hit the exact octave shown on the staff

PLAY THE BUILT-IN PIANO
• Two-octave keyboard with black keys and optional key labels
• Relative or exact answering, just like microphone mode

SMART PRACTICE THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
• Repeat mistakes: the notes you miss show up more often
• Big leaps: train reading across large intervals and ledger lines
• Score and streak tracking keep you motivated

SIMPLE AND RESPECTFUL
• Works completely offline
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• Tiny download size

Noteex is supported by donations. If it helps you, you can buy the developer a coffee: https://ko-fi.com/simelab
```
